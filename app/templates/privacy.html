Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

This page describes what personal information the Customer Database service handles, why, and who it is shared with.

What we handle

  • Staff account details: a username, a hashed password, and, if you sign in with Google, your Google account ID and email address.
  • Customer records: names, email addresses, phone numbers, companies, postal addresses and notes that staff enter.
  • Verification requests: when DocuSign checks a name, the name is compared with the customer records. The result, and the matched customer's ID, email and company, are returned to DocuSign. The signer is shown only a generic result.
  • Service logs: the request path, response status, the requesting network address, and the reason an access attempt was refused, such as an email address that is not on the allowed list. Access tokens are never logged.

Why we use it

To sign staff in, to keep customer records, to answer verification requests, and to keep the service secure and working.

Who we share it with

  • Google: to sign staff in, and to check the access tokens that DocuSign presents.
  • DocuSign: the verification results described above, when the DocuSign extension is used.
  • Our hosting providers: Railway, which runs the application and its database.

We do not sell personal information.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Sessions use signed cookies, and in production the service is served over HTTPS. Access to the API from outside the staff web interface is limited to approved accounts.
